Jumping: hold Space to preload, release just before the lip: the push takes about a tenth of a second, so the ramp gives the height and your timing adds the rest.
While loaded, WSAD shift your weight and change how you leave the ramp.
In the air everything has momentum (let go and you keep rotating; steer the other way to stop):
AD flat spin · S backflip · W frontflip · Shift+AD roll · T tabletop
↑ flair · ↑+AD cork · ↑+AD+S rodeo · ↑+AD+W misty
F tuck (spin faster) · hold Space in the air to spread out (slows rotation)
↓ tailwhip · ←→ barspin · Q superman · E can-can
Let go of the keys before you land. The bike gets a light nudge toward the landing (Settings → landing assist).
